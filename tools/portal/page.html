Lua API cheat sheet
functions you define (draw is required):
  init()   update(dt) -> true to redraw   draw()
  touch(kind, x, y) kind "down"|"move"|"up"   stop()
drawing (screen 320x240, colours 0xRRGGBB):
  gfx.clear(c)  gfx.rect(x,y,w,h,c[,radius])
  gfx.circle(x,y,r,c)  gfx.line(x1,y1,x2,y2,c[,thick])
  gfx.text(s,x,y,c[,size 1|2[,"l"|"c"|"r"]])  (y = centre)
  gfx.measure(s[,size])  gfx.rgb(r,g,b)  gfx.redraw()
the robot (apps are part of the face):
  gfx.face(x,y,width)  draws the live face, centred on (x,y)
  face.event(name[,k])  pet praise love greet poke annoy scold startle win fail tired confuse bore  (3 a second)
  face.say(text)  up to 7 letters beside the face    face.urge(name)  the mind net's wish, -1..1
  face.emote(name)  heart star dizzy cry sweat bubble angry question exclaim music laugh love think shy rage money glitch sparkle dead  (1 a second)
  face.mood() -> valence, arousal, dominance
other:
  sys.ms()  sys.time() -> h,m,s,day,month,year,weekday
  sys.light()  sys.log(...)  sys.exit()  sys.capture(true)
  store.get(k[,default])  store.set(k,v)   (16 keys, remembered)
  http.get("http://...", function(status, body) end)
BOOT button (not available to apps) = next screen, long press = back to the face; hold a finger still 2.5 s = back to the face
limits: 40 KB memory, 40 ms per call, 256 draw commands

Diary

Weeks

From the SD card's log. Each bar is a day: the height is how much happened, green the care it got, red the hard moments.
Each row is a day; the 24 boxes are the hours from midnight (green = happy, red = unhappy, grey = no data).

Mind net

Left: what it senses. Middle: its inner state. Right: what it wants. Lines: the strongest causes.

Temperament and needs

Bars: what it wants to do right now. It starts from sensible instincts, wanders a little on its own, and learns from your reactions: petting and praise reinforce what it was doing, pokes and scolding discourage it.

Packs and backup

A pack holds looks, a behaviour file, apps and pictures. A full backup also holds its settings and what it has learned: keep that one private. Neither holds Wi-Fi details or the PIN.
